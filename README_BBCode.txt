[size=6][b]RE4CabbyCodes[/b][/size]

A [b]cheat panel[/b] for [b]Resident Evil 4[/b] (the 2023 remake) on Steam. Pause the game and a panel appears beside the pause menu; beside the game's save and load lists it manages your save files, beside its challenges screen your challenges, and beside its Extra Content Shop the shop's items.

This mod is open source! Check it out at [url=https://github.com/SirCabby/RE4CabbyCodes]https://github.com/SirCabby/RE4CabbyCodes[/url]

[size=5][b]What it does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]God mode[/b] - you take no damage, and your health is held at its maximum. You still flinch and grabs still play out; deaths the game scripts without a hit (a fall, a missed QTE) are left to it. Luis is covered too while he fights beside you (no damage, his health held at its maximum), and in Separate Ways the time Ada has to save him from the flames never runs out (its gauge stays full). The water scooter of the escape at the end takes no damage either, so no crash sinks it (finishing the ride undamaged earns the game's Smooth Escape challenge).
[*][b]Ashley takes no damage[/b] - the same for Ashley, and a hit no longer puts her down. Being carried off is not damage.
[*][b]One hit kills[/b] - any hit that does damage kills an enemy, through the game's own death (drops and parasites as usual; a parasite that bursts out of a head dies to one hit too). An enemy the game keeps alive for now (a boss between phases) keeps its last point.
[*][b]Infinite ammo[/b] - firing uses no rounds, bolts or arrows, and a thrown grenade stays in the case.
[*][b]No durability loss[/b] - a knife never wears down.
[*][b]No armor durability loss[/b] - your body armor never wears down: the hits it takes for you, and fire and frost, cost it nothing, so it never breaks. An armor durability editor, which also mends broken armor.
[*][b]Steady aim[/b] - your aim holds still: no recoil kick or jolt when you fire, no sway or shake while you aim or look down a scope (the view and your arms hold still), and the reticle is always fully focused, so shots go where it points (no random spread; a shotgun keeps its pellet pattern).
[*][b]Automatic QTEs[/b] - every timed prompt is taken for you: the dodge and knife prompts of enemies and bosses, the boulder, the laser corridors, Ada's hookshot prompt and the lever with Ashley. A grab you can break free from is broken at once (by mashing, or with the knife where only the knife frees you), and the swim back to the boat fills by itself. Ordinary parries are left to you.
[*][b]Automatic shooting gallery[/b] - the merchant's shooting gallery plays itself: every target that scores is hit as it shows and every medal is taken, never a sailor. Each game ends with the top rating and its rewards.
[*][b]Change costumes in game[/b] - the game only lets you change costumes from the main menu (the pause menu's EXTRAS changes accessories only). With this on, EXTRAS changes costumes too, under the game's rules for accessories: not during combat or a cutscene.
[*][b]Difficulty[/b] - switch between Assisted, Standard, Hardcore and Professional on the spot (main game and Separate Ways).
[*][b]The Mercenaries[/b] (shown only there) - [b]Endless combo[/b] (a combo never runs out, and what it earns goes onto your score as it is earned), [b]Mercenary Mode always on[/b] (its gauge always full, and once started it never runs out), [b]Kill enemies as they appear[/b] (every enemy dies the moment it is in play, wherever it is, to a shot of yours from afar: the game's own death, and the kill scores as yours do - its points, the combo, the kill count and the time a kill earns; the big ones as well; a Ganado carrying a parasite dies before the parasite can come out, and one already out dies as soon as it is; every part of the stage keeps sending enemies, wherever you stand; a stage holds 150 enemies in all, in waves as the kills mount, and ends the moment the last one is down - a few minutes in with this on; a Ganado hiding in a closet still waits for you to come near; it waits for the clock to start and stops when time is up or you are down; it lasts one match - it switches itself off when the match ends, so switch it on again in the next), and [b]Freeze countdown timer[/b] (the clock stops where it is, so time never runs out; time bonuses still add to it).
[*][b]Saves, time & money[/b] - [b]Save without counting[/b] and a save count editor; [b]Freeze play time[/b] and a play time editor (the time the game shows and records, which the clear's rank goes by, and the RESULTS screen's total play time with it; a death and Continue add nothing to it); [b]Freeze countdown timer[/b] and a countdown editor for timed scenes; [b]Infinite money[/b] (the merchant takes no pesetas; selling still pays) and a pesetas editor; [b]Infinite spinels[/b] (the merchant takes no spinels for a trade; the spinels you find still add up) and a spinels editor; [b]Infinite upgrade tickets[/b] (an exclusive upgrade paid with an Exclusive Upgrade Ticket keeps the ticket, so one ticket does every weapon's; a ticket bought as DLC is still used up) and a tickets editor (up to 999).
[*][b]Results[/b] - [b]Misses don't count[/b], [b]Deaths don't count[/b], [b]Damage doesn't count[/b], and editors for the deaths, times damaged, kills, hits and misses - this chapter's and the whole game's (a lower whole-game count takes the latest finished chapters down).
[*][b]Attache case editor[/b] - change counts, a gun's loaded rounds, a knife's durability and a weapon's upgrades (including the exclusive upgrade), throw items away, or make the case bigger.
[*][b]Add to the case[/b] - every item that takes room in the case, under tabs and headings with a search box. The game's own placement finds it room.
[*][b]Key items[/b], [b]Treasures[/b] and [b]Other items[/b] (recipes, charms, treasure maps, case skins, armor) - each list sorted under headings for its kinds, with remove, counts and its own Add.
[*][b]Many at once[/b] - tick several items in any Add list (or Tick all listed), say how many of each, and add them all; tick items in a list (All, or a heading, ticks a whole list or kind) to store, take, throw away or remove them together; change several counts and Apply all. Each is one confirmation for the lot.
[*][b]Item box[/b] - a limitless item box kept in your save, for anything that takes room in the case: Store and Take, weapons keeping their upgrades, rounds and durability. Weapons, attachments and First Aid Sprays go into the game's own storage, so a typewriter's Storage has them too; everything else is kept beside it, in the same save. Each save has its own box: a load brings back the box that save had, and a new game starts with an empty one. The Mercenaries has none.
[*][b]Save files[/b] - beside the title's Load list, the pause menu's, the game over screen's and a typewriter's save list: [b]Delete[/b] a save, or [b]Copy[/b] one into another slot of the same game (empty or not). The game itself writes the copy, so it shows on the list at once and stays after a restart.
[*][b]Challenges[/b] - beside the game's challenges screen (Bonuses in the main menu, or the pause menu): switch each challenge of the main story and Separate Ways on or off, under the game's own tabs, with a search box and Complete all / Clear all. Switched on, a challenge is complete as if you had earned it: it pays its CP, what it unlocks can be bought in the Extra Content Shop (Professional mode opens straight away), and a challenge with a Steam achievement unlocks it. Switched off, it can be earned again: its CP is taken back and its shop items are locked again, unless you bought them or something else unlocks them too. Steam keeps achievements. Every change is saved at once.
[*][b]Extra Content Shop[/b] - beside the game's Extra Content Shop (Bonuses in the main menu): unlock any of the shop's items, under the shop's own tabs, with a search box and Unlock all / Lock all. An unlocked item is for sale in the shop, and you buy it there with your CP as usual - nothing is bought for you and your CP is left alone. Untick an item to lock it again (not one you have bought). The shop's own list shows each change at once, and every change is saved at once.
[*][b]F7[/b] hides or shows the panel beside the screen you are on; it is back by itself the next time that screen opens. In play, F7 opens the cheat panel over the game. Out of a game (the title, the main menu) there is no panel. The cheat switches are remembered between starts, and so is each window's place, size and what you folded in it (the window itself or its sections).
[/list]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]

Works the same on Windows and on Linux (Proton), with no launch options. REFramework can stay installed.

Open the game's folder (in Steam: right-click the game > Manage > Browse local files):
[list=1]
[*]Rename [b]steam_api64.dll[/b] to [b]steam_api64_orig.dll[/b]
[*]Copy this mod's [b]steam_api64.dll[/b] in beside it
[/list]

To uninstall: delete steam_api64.dll and rename steam_api64_orig.dll back.

Made for the current Steam version of the game (build 22377325). On another version the mod says so and leaves the game alone.

[size=5][b]Please read before using[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]This first version has not been played with yet: everything it uses was found in the game's own code and checked against it, but expect rough edges, and report them with the log.
[*]Back up your saves before using the save file manager (Steam/userdata/<your id>/2050650/remote/). Delete and Copy cannot be undone. A copy made with an earlier version of the mod that shows as empty: copy it again.
[*]Item and save changes are made only while the game is paused, challenges only while the game's challenges screen is open, the shop's items only while the Extra Content Shop is open.
[*]The item box is saved with the game, like the attache case: a change is kept by your next save and lost if you quit without saving. Before removing the mod, take out what you stored: what the game's storage does not normally hold shows up in it at your next load, one of each item.
[/list]

[size=5][b]If it does not work[/b][/size]

The mod says why - in a message box as the game starts, or in the panel. The usual fix: delete steam_api64_orig.dll, verify the game's files in Steam (Properties > Installed Files > Verify integrity), then do the install steps again. Otherwise send [b]RE4CabbyCodes.log[/b] and [b]RE4CabbyCodes.prev.log[/b] from the game's folder.

[size=5][b]Config[/b][/size]

[code]
ToggleKey             = 0x76  ; the key that hides/shows the panel (0x76 = F7)
GodMode               = 0     ; each cheat's state when the game starts - the panel writes changes back here
AshleyGodMode         = 0
OneHitKills           = 0
InfiniteAmmo          = 0
NoDurabilityLoss      = 0
NoArmorDurabilityLoss = 0
SteadyAim             = 0
AutoQTE               = 0
AutoGallery           = 0
InGameCostumes        = 0
FreezePlaytime        = 0
FreezeCountdown       = 0
NoSaveCount           = 0
InfiniteMoney         = 0
InfiniteSpinels       = 0
InfiniteTickets       = 0
NoMissCount           = 0
NoDeathCount          = 0
NoDamageCount         = 0
EndlessCombo          = 0
MercenaryMode         = 0
AutoKill              = 0
[/code]

Lives in [b]RE4CabbyCodes.ini[/b] beside the DLL (created on the first start). The item box lives in your saves. An item box file from an earlier version ([b]RE4CabbyCodes.itembox.ini[/b], [b]RE4CabbyCodes.itembox.separate-ways.ini[/b]), or one a save could not keep (...unsaved.ini), is offered in the panel's Item box: [b]Move into this game[/b] puts its items in the game you have loaded, and the file goes once your next save keeps them - no copy is left. Quit without saving and the file stays, offered again.
